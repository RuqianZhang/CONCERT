===============================================
Data Dictionary for LendingClub Dataset
===============================================

This file provides brief descriptions of the variables used in the study
(excluding interaction terms). The dataset is a subset of the publicly
available LendingClub loan records (2007–2011).

Variable Descriptions:
----------------------

purpose:
    Stated purpose of the loan (e.g., wedding, medical, moving).

loan_status:
    Current status of the loan (1 = paid, 0 = default).

loan_amnt:
    The total loan amount funded by investors (USD).

term:
    Number of payments on the loan (1 = 60 months, 0 = 36 months).

int_rate:
    Annual interest rate on the loan (%).

installment:
    Monthly payment owed by the borrower.

emp_length:
    Employment length of the borrower (in years).

annual_inc:
    Annual income of the borrower (USD).

verification_status:
    Indicates whether the borrower’s income was verified (1 = verified, 0 = not verified).

dti:
    Debt-to-income ratio of the borrower (ratio of the monthly payments on the total debt obligations, excluding mortgage, to self-reported monthly income).

delinq_2yrs:
    Number of delinquency in the borrower’s credit file for the past 2 years.

inq_last_6mths:
    Number of inquiries by creditors during the past 6 months.

open_acc:
    Number of open credit lines in the borrower’s credit file.

pub_rec:
    Number of derogatory public records.

revol_bal:
    Total revolving credit balance (USD).

revol_util:
    Revolving line utilization rate, or the amount of credit the borrower is using relative to all available revolving credit.

total_acc:
    Total number of credit lines currently in the borrower’s credit file.

pub_rec_bankruptcies:
    Number of public record bankruptcies.

grade:
    Credit grade assigned by the platform (transformed to numbers from A–G).

fico:
    FICO credit score of the borrower.

home_ownership_OWN:
    Dummy variable indicating the borrower owns their home.

home_ownership_RENT:
    Dummy variable indicating the borrower rents their home.
